FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How long does it take to build and deploy a custom AI agent?

Most custom agents are designed, trained on your data, and deployed into sandbox testing within 7 to 14 business days.

Is our company data kept completely private?

Yes, 100%. We enforce zero-retention policies. Your private company documents and CRM data are never used to train public LLM models.

What happens if the AI agent encounters an unknown question?

Our agents feature strict confidence thresholds. If an inquiry falls below threshold, the agent seamlessly hands off the conversation to a human team member via Slack, CRM, or Zendesk.
